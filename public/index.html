THE UNDERTOW

A Fishing Town, Est. 1891 — Reopened for Residency

The port has been dredged, the ferry line restored, and the old lodgings aired out at last. Thanks to the generosity of the Coddiwomple Maritime Co., The Undertow welcomes new residents and travelers to its shores after a long, quiet stretch.

01 — Welcome

A Town That Waited

The Undertow has stood on this headland since before the maps agreed on its name. Fog rolls in most mornings and rarely fully lifts. The tide runs stranger here than the almanacs account for.

For six long years the Authority kept the gates closed to newcomers — no incidents to speak of, they'll tell you, and none worth mentioning since. Now, with the new port complete, the town opens its doors once more.

Settle a cottage on the bluff. Take up a trade on the docks. Just mind the harbor bell after dark, and don't go asking the old-timers what happened to the last new family.

02 — A Brief History

Ledger of the Undertow

Kept, imperfectly, by successive Harbor Authorities. Some entries are missing. This is not an oversight.

1891

The Undertow founded on a natural deepwater cove. First lighthouse lit the same winter.

1930s

Cannery years. The town's only true boom. Population peaks; the docks are rebuilt twice.

1968

The old ferry line is discontinued without public explanation. Twenty-three names added to the harbor memorial.

Present

Port reopened with Coddiwomple funding. New residency applications accepted for the first time in years.
